## About me

Software Design student at TH Aschaffenburg specializing in **data science, analytics, and AI** with a bias for taking ideas all the way from messy raw data and trained models to tools people can actually use.

I spend my time building practical systems with Python, SQL, Java, LightGBM, FastAPI, Docker, and CI/CD, with a strong interest in responsible AI and AI policy—fair, explainable models and the guardrails around them, such as the EU AI Act.

* **Currently building: Glassbox Credit**, an EU AI Act-aligned, explainable credit-scoring system complete with bias audits, counterfactuals, and bilingual (EN/DE) decision letters.

* **Off the keyboard:** Reading philosophy, paddling or rowing when the water cooperates, and indulging in good movies.

* **Internship Radar:** A 20–26 week *Pflichtpraktikum* starting **February 2027** (English or German) across data science/AI, backend/software engineering, or DevOps/security.
